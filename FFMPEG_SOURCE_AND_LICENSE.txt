FFprobe distribution notice
===========================

The portable CleanSpace delivery includes ffprobe.exe from the locally installed
Gyan.dev FFmpeg 9.0 full build. The executable reports GPL version 3 or later.

FFmpeg project: https://ffmpeg.org/
License information: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
Gyan.dev builds and source-code links: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/

CleanSpace does not modify FFprobe. It invokes FFprobe only to inspect video
container readability and duration.
